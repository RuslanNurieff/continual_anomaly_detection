
# Continual Learning for Visual Anomaly Detection

This messy repository contains the thesis work materials, experiments, etc. for my master's thesis at the University of Padova (you can access the thesis by Googling "Ruslan Nuriev UniPd thesis").

While the experiments are too unstructured, I believe that the core ideas are fine (fine in the sense that it's foundations were strong at first, then drifted away...), like continual data streaming, engine for continual models, etc. ("cl" folder). And these (mainly initial design choices) were partly used in continual learning framework of MoViAD library.

Thanks to Davide Dalle Pezze, Manuel Barusco, and Francesco Borsatti for their incredible guidance (mainly theoretical) through the work. I am yet to hear or see this level of guidance from other supervisors.
